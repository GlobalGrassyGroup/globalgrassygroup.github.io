Kundelungu National Park, located in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, is a biodiversity hotspot. Established in 1970 to protect the region's unique ecosystems, including the cheetah and Burchell's zebra, the park is home to an impressive variety of flora and fauna. In May 2026, our team had the privilege of conducting a research mission in this fascinating area, focused on plant diversity in the wet savannas at the springhead of the Lutshipuka River. This mission aimed to establish a system of permanent plots to study the biodiversity of the wet savannas.
